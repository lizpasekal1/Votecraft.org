The card catalog

Everything the library actually does.

No filler features — this is the real drawer, top to bottom.

No. 01

Dashboard

A hero collage built from your own saved covers greets you on open, alongside a peek at your queue, your favorites, and the curated genre chips.

No. 02

My Saves Queue

A real board — Queue, In Progress, My Notes, Archive. Drag a save across as you move through it; reorder within a column any time.

No. 03

Curated SaveCraft

Real Top 100 lists — Rolling Stone's musicians, the New York Times' movies, Steam's games — browsable by genre. No guesswork, real attribution.

No. 04

Embed Builder

Turn a whole folder or a hand-picked list into a styled slider — pick slide count, spacing, autoplay, and a font, then copy the embed link.

No. 05

Share & export

Send a saved list as a link, on or off at will — or pull your own library out as CSV or Markdown whenever you want it in plain text.

No. 06

Right-click to save

Right-click any page → Save to SaveCraft, image and all. Or click the toolbar icon for a two-tap add without leaving the tab.
