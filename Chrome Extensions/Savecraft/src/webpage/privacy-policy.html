🔒 Privacy

Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 12, 2026

⚠️ This is a working draft, written to accurately describe what SaveCraft actually does today — not final legal text. Have it reviewed before treating it as your published policy, and update it any time the app's data handling changes.

SaveCraft ("SaveCraft," "we," "us") is a personal media library — you save links to movies, shows, music, books, and games you want to experience, organize them into folders, and optionally share them. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have. Your use of SaveCraft is also subject to our Terms of Service.

1. How SaveCraft Collects Information

Account information. If you create an account, that's an email address and password, handled by Google's Firebase Authentication — we never see or store your raw password ourselves.

Content you save. The titles, links, images, notes, and folder/category organization you create while using SaveCraft.

Optional connections. If you connect a Last.fm or Steam account, we store the username/ID you provide and read your public profile activity (recent scrobbles or recently played games). Neither connection ever requests a password or login.

Page information at save time. When you save a page, we read that page's title, description, and preview image to build your saved card — this only happens for pages you actively choose to save, never for general browsing.

Device and usage data. Like most web services, our infrastructure (Google Firebase) may log standard technical information such as IP address, browser type, and request timestamps for security and reliability purposes.

2. How SaveCraft Uses Information

We use the information above to:

  • Provide, operate, and sync your saved library across devices;
  • Communicate with you, such as email verification or account-related notices;
  • Maintain the security and integrity of the service.

We only use your information where we have a real reason to: because you've asked us to (for example, creating an account, or connecting Last.fm/Steam), because it's genuinely necessary to provide the Service you're using, because we're legally required to, or because we have a legitimate interest in doing so — in particular, a legitimate interest in keeping SaveCraft secure, understanding how it's used so we can improve it, and reporting on how curated content performs for the partners behind it. Where we're relying on your consent (like an optional connection you've turned on), you can withdraw it at any time — for example, by disconnecting Last.fm/Steam from your Profile page — and we'll stop from that point forward; this doesn't undo anything that already happened while consent was in place.

Analytics. We may use your information to understand and analyze how people use SaveCraft, so we can improve it.

Curated content & partner reporting. SaveCraft features curated lists from nonprofit and advocacy partners. We may share aggregate usage information about that content — for example, how a curated list is performing overall — with the partner behind it. This is reporting on content, not on you: it is not tied back to your individual account or identity.

3. How SaveCraft Discloses Information

We do not sell or rent your personal information. We may disclose information where required by law, in response to valid legal process, or where we believe in good faith it's necessary to protect the rights, property, or safety of SaveCraft, our users, or the public. Aggregate, non-identifying data may be shared as described in "Curated content & partner reporting" above.

4. International Data Transfers

SaveCraft's infrastructure (Google Firebase) is based in the United States. If you're using SaveCraft from outside the U.S., your information will be transferred to and processed in the United States, which may not have the same data protection laws as your home country. By using SaveCraft, you consent to that transfer and processing as described in this policy.

5. Where Your Data Lives

Everything is stored in your browser's own local storage by default. If you sign in, your saves, folders, and account settings are also synced to Firestore, Google's cloud database — access is restricted so that only your authenticated account can reach your own data, enforced by the database itself rather than by our own application code.

SaveCraft's website (savecraft.org) and Chrome extension read from and write to this same account system — signing in on one works on the other, and your saves stay in sync between them.

6. Sharing Your Saves

SaveCraft's Share feature lets you generate a link to a saved list. Anyone with that link can see the items in that specific list — it does not expose your account email, password, or any other saved list you haven't chosen to share. You can turn link sharing off at any time from the share menu.

7. Third-Party Services

To find covers, descriptions, and other details for things you save, SaveCraft looks things up from public catalogs and metadata sources — for example iTunes, Open Library, MusicBrainz, Wikipedia, Steam, and YouTube. These lookups are based only on what you're actively searching for or saving, not on your identity or browsing history, and none of your account information is sent to them.

8. Data Retention

We keep your information for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide the service. If you delete your account, we delete your saved items, folders, and settings from our systems, except where we're required to retain information for legal or dispute-resolution purposes.

9. Our Commitment to Security

We take reasonable measures to protect your information, including access rules that restrict your data to your own authenticated account. That said, no method of storage or transmission over the internet is 100% secure, and we can't guarantee absolute security.

10. Children's Privacy

SaveCraft is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect information from them. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, contact us and we'll delete it.

11. Do Not Track

Some browsers offer a "Do Not Track" signal. Because there's no common industry standard for how to interpret it, SaveCraft does not currently respond to Do Not Track signals.

12. Your Rights and Choices

General. You can access, update, or delete the content you've saved at any time within SaveCraft. You can permanently delete your account and all associated data from Profile → Manage account → Delete account — this can't be undone, and doesn't reach into anything still cached locally in that specific browser (clearing your browser's site data for SaveCraft covers that).

If you're in the EU, UK, or a similar region. You may have additional rights over your personal information, such as requesting a copy of it or asking us to correct or delete it. Contact us below to make a request — we're a small, pre-launch project and don't yet have a formal process for every jurisdiction's specific rights, but we'll do our best to help.

13. Changes to This Policy

If how SaveCraft handles data changes in a meaningful way, this page will be updated and the "Last updated" date above will reflect it.
